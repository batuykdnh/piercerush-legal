Privacy Policy for Pierce Rush

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pierce Rush, developed and published by Yeko Games, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the game.

Summary: Pierce Rush does not require you to provide your real name, email address, phone number, or other direct contact information to play. Analytics is optional and is only enabled when you explicitly allow it in the game.

1. Information We Process

Account and Authentication Data

Pierce Rush uses Unity Authentication and may use Google Play Games Services to authenticate players and link game progress to a Google Play Games account.

This may include:

  • A Unity Authentication Player ID
  • A Google Play Games player/account identifier
  • Authentication tokens or authorization codes required to securely sign in and link accounts

Pierce Rush does not receive or store your Google account password.

Game Progress and Cloud Data

If online services are available, game progress may be stored using Unity Gaming Services. This can include information such as:

  • High scores
  • Game progress
  • In-game currency
  • Upgrades
  • Equipped character or cosmetic selections
  • Tutorial and progression state
  • Leaderboard-related game statistics

Optional Analytics

Pierce Rush uses Unity Analytics to help understand how the game is played and to improve the game.

Analytics collection is optional. On first launch, you may choose whether to allow Analytics. You can play Pierce Rush normally without allowing Analytics, and you can change your choice later in the game's Settings menu.

When Analytics is allowed, Unity may process information such as:

  • Installation-specific identifiers
  • Unity Authentication Player ID, when available
  • IP address
  • Device and application information
  • Gameplay interactions and events, such as starting or completing a run

If Analytics consent is denied, Pierce Rush does not intentionally send gameplay Analytics events through Unity Analytics.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate players
  • Save and restore game progress
  • Provide leaderboards and online game functionality
  • Maintain account and cloud-save synchronization
  • Prevent data loss and maintain service reliability
  • Understand game usage and improve Pierce Rush when Analytics has been allowed

3. Third-Party Services

Pierce Rush uses services provided by third parties to operate online features. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Unity Technologies — Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Cloud Code and, when permitted by the player, Unity Analytics.
  • Google LLC — Google Play Games Services for supported Google Play Games authentication and account functionality.

For more information, please review the privacy documentation provided by Unity Technologies and Google.

4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The current version of Pierce Rush does not display advertisements and does not offer in-app purchases.

5. Analytics Consent and Your Choices

Analytics is disabled unless the required Analytics consent has been granted. You may allow or deny Analytics when prompted.

You may later change your Analytics preference from the Settings menu. Turning Analytics off prevents future Analytics collection through the game's Analytics integration.

6. Data Storage and Security

Online information is transmitted to the service providers required to provide Pierce Rush's online functionality. We use reasonable technical measures and the security mechanisms provided by Unity Gaming Services and Google Play Services to protect information during transmission and processing.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Game account and cloud-save information may be retained while your game account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide the requested online services.

Unity states that personal data processed by Unity Analytics is retained for 13 months by default, subject to Unity's current policies and applicable configuration.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Pierce Rush provides an account deletion option inside the game's Settings menu. You may use this option to request deletion of your Pierce Rush account and associated supported cloud data.

You may also request deletion of your account or associated data by contacting:

Email: YOUR_REAL_EMAIL

When contacting us, please provide enough information to identify the relevant Pierce Rush account, such as your Player ID when available.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate technical purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Pierce Rush is not directed at children. The intended Google Play target audience for the game is adults aged 18 and over.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its online services, or legal requirements change.

Changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
